# Code of conduct

Aven follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact details on the turin-dev GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for reports. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
